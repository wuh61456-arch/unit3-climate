Speaker A"So, how is the world fighting back? We have signed global agreements like the Kyoto Protocol and the Paris Agreement. They are historic milestones."
Speaker B"But the reality is a struggle. The global response is fragmented. It's like a group project where everyone agrees on the goal, but some members refuse to share their work or resources. Some countries even withdrew due to economic interests. It makes unified action very hard."
